The decision

Every enterprise is under pressure to add AI to the applications that run the business. The usual approach connects an AI model directly to production systems and data. That approach is risky, because an AI model that makes a mistake can damage the records the business depends on. It is expensive, because AI providers charge for every piece of data the model reads. It also exposes sensitive data to the model.

Fractal takes a different approach. It builds a digital twin: a live, continuously updated copy of your existing systems, running on inexpensive hardware you control. AI works only on the twin, so your production systems cannot be harmed. The AI model only translates requests into instructions and turns results into plain-language summaries, so it never sees your private data and your AI costs stay small.

Starting with that first, low-risk step, Fractal can go on to provide a hot backup of your existing applications and, when you are ready, replace them with faster, lower-cost Fractal versions. The first step is a 90-day deployment at production scale that runs alongside your existing systems.

The hidden risk: AI with access to your systems of record

Enterprise AI applications such as billing automation, customer care and transaction processing need read/write access to databases and systems of record to do anything useful. That access is also the source of a critical, often overlooked risk.

AI systems are complex and non-deterministic. Prompt injection, model hallucinations, conflicts between concurrent agents and unexpected edge cases can all corrupt, overwrite or destroy data. A single bad write to a billing database can mean wrong charges to millions of customers. The consequences can be immediate, widespread and career-ending.

A safe, controlled path to enterprise AI

You don't have to bet the business on a cutover. Each stage delivers value on its own, and your existing applications keep running until you decide to retire them.

  1. Stage 1 · within 90 days

    Add AI on a digital twin

    Fractal builds a twin of your existing systems and adds AI capabilities to it. The twin runs in parallel with production and is measured against it every day.

    Result: safe AI capabilities, at production scale
  2. Stage 2

    Use the twin as a hot backup

    Because the twin stays in sync with production, it can take over if an existing system fails. It provides resilience you would otherwise have to buy separately.

    Result: failover at 1/10 the cost
  3. Stage 3 · on your timeline

    Replace with the Fractal version

    Once the Fractal version has proven itself against live data, move workloads to it and retire the original. There is no hard cutover date.

    Result: ≥ 50% lower cost, ≥ 10× faster

Why it is low risk: at every stage the existing applications remain in place and unchanged. Decisions to move forward are based on results measured against your own production data, not on projections.
